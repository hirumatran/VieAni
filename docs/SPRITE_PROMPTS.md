# VieAni – illustration prompt pack (batch 1: V001–V005)

Art direction (PO, 13 Nov 2026): **clean 2D creature illustration** – smooth line art, soft cel shading,
like official monster-collecting-game art – NOT pixel art. Designs must be our own (style reference only;
never ask for or copy any existing Pokémon/character).

## How to use (Gemini or any image generator)
1. Paste **STYLE** + one **SUBJECT** + one **VIEW** as one prompt (English works best).
2. Generate the **FRONT** view first. Pick the best of 2–4 tries.
3. For the **BACK** view, attach the chosen front image and add: *"Same character as the attached image, same colours and markings."*
4. Save as `V001_front.png`, `V001_back.png`, … (square, at least 1024×1024) and put them in a Drive folder
   or upload to the repo folder `assets/illustrations/raw/`.
5. Claude then removes the background, scales all VieAni consistently, and builds the in-game animations
   (idle, attack, skill cast, hit, ★2 silver outline, ★3 golden glow) – no extra drawings needed.

How views are used: **FRONT** = VieAni on the opponent's board (faces the player). **BACK** = VieAni on our board
(we see it from behind, facing the enemy).

## STYLE (same for every VieAni)
> Original creature character design for a 2D monster-collecting strategy game, official character art style:
> clean smooth dark-brown line art with varying line weight, flat colours with soft cel shading (2–3 tones per
> colour), light coming from the top-left, a little soft watercolor texture, friendly but strong expressive eyes,
> slightly stylised and chunky proportions, readable silhouette. Full body, whole creature visible, centered, about
> 10% empty margin around it, plain pure white background, no ground shadow, no text, no logo, no frame, no humans,
> no other characters.

## VIEW
- **FRONT:** *Three-quarter front view: the creature faces the viewer and turns slightly to the left, standing in a confident ready-to-fight pose.*
- **BACK:** *Three-quarter back view: we see the creature from behind and slightly to the left, its back and the back of its head, facing up and to the right as if looking at an opponent in the distance; same ready-to-fight pose.*

## Tips
- If it adds a background or ground: add *"isolated on pure white background"* again at the end.
- If it looks too realistic: add *"more stylised, cartoon proportions, bigger head and eyes"*.
- If it looks too childish: add *"slightly more mature and heroic"*.
- Keep the same chat for all 5 so the style stays consistent; if a later one drifts, attach V001_front as a style reference.
- Licence note: in many countries purely AI-generated images get no copyright – check before a commercial release (task T39).
