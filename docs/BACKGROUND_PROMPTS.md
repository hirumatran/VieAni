# VieAni – battle background prompts (Gemini)

Art direction (PO decision, 11 Nov 2026): **soft painted backgrounds + pixel-art VieAni** on top,
like the PO's Gemini reference of Vịnh Hạ Long. Backgrounds are generated by the PO with Gemini,
then Claude prepares them for the game (crop to 16:9, resize, colour-match, soften the board area).
The procedural pixel drafts in `assets/backgrounds/` stay as placeholders until then.

## How to use
1. Paste the **style block** + one **scene line** into Gemini as one prompt.
2. Ask for **16:9, as large as possible** (at least 1920×1080).
3. Generate 2–4 variants per scene, keep the best one, save as `bg_<id>.png`
   (ids: ha_long, phong_nha, nam_cat_tien, mu_cang_chai, hoan_kiem, trang_an, tram_chim, con_dao).
4. Put them in one Google Drive folder and share the link (or upload in the chat).

## Style block (same for all 8 – keeps the set consistent)
> Soft painted digital illustration for a 2D strategy game battle background, wide 16:9 landscape.
> Gentle painterly brushwork, smooth colour gradients, atmospheric mist and depth, soft natural
> daylight, slightly stylised but recognisable real Vietnamese place. Calm, uncluttered middle area
> (the game board will be placed over the centre), more detail toward the left and right edges.
> Eye level slightly above the scene, horizon in the upper third. No people, no animals, no text,
> no logos, no user interface, no game board, no frame.

## Tips
- If Gemini adds animals, people or a board anyway, ask: "same image, remove all animals, people and boards".
- If the middle is too busy: "make the centre calmer and less detailed".
- Note: in many countries purely AI-generated images get no copyright, so others could reuse them;
  check Google's current terms before a commercial release (task T39 legal check).
